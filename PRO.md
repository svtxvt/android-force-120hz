# Force 120Hz Pro (planned)

**Status: not built yet.** I am checking whether enough people want it before I spend the time. The free app stays free and open.

Most people find this project because refresh-rate **jumps** on LTPO screens (1–10 Hz snapping back to 120 Hz) strain their eyes. The free app keeps the panel at its maximum rate. Pro is meant for people who need a **constant rate of their choice**.

## Planned

- **Rate lock at 120 Hz or at 60 Hz**, held constant, on devices where the system honours it.
- **Per-app lock**: for example a 60 Hz lock in reader apps and nothing in games.
- **Auto-off** below a battery level you choose.
- **Quick Settings tile.**
- **A tested-devices list** built from user reports, so you know before you install.

One-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no network permission (the free app has none either, and it cannot read what is on your screen).
